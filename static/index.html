Automatic Application Policy & Rules

Candidate data protection, eligibility criteria, and safety controls
🎯
1. Strict > 70% Skill-Match Threshold

Applications are eligible for automatic submission only when the normalized skill-match score is strictly greater than 70%. A score of exactly 70% or below is placed in the manual review queue and will never be automatically submitted.

🛡️
2. Mandatory Eligibility Checks Required

Before any auto-submission, the engine verifies that the candidate satisfies all mandatory qualifications. Any detected qualification gaps disqualify the vacancy from automatic submission and route it to MANUAL_ACTION_REQUIRED.

📄
3. Profile-Specific Document Generation

Submissions automatically use the selected profile's tailored ATS resume (AI Engineer PDF vs Test Engineer PDF) and an individualized cover letter generated with the actual current date, company name, and vacancy title.

⚠️
4. Direct Employer Transmission Notice

When automatic submission is enabled, personal information (full name, phone, email, location, LinkedIn/GitHub) and application documents are sent directly to employer ATS platforms without individual manual approval.

🔒
5. Duplicate Prevention & Historical Audit

Unique fingerprint tracking across company, title, and job URL prevents duplicate submissions to the same role. All submission responses, references, and timestamps are recorded in the database and Excel tracker.

❌
6. Accurate Failure Tracking

If an employer's application system rejects or fails during transmission, the status is recorded as SUBMISSION_FAILED with full diagnostic error details. It is never falsely recorded as APPLIED.

🤖
7. Bot Protection & Restricted Portals

The system does not bypass CAPTCHAs, two-factor authentication, or restricted portal workflows. Such vacancies are automatically flagged for manual candidate completion.

⚙️
8. Independent Controls & Daily Limits

Automatic submission can be enabled or disabled independently for AI Engineer and Test Engineer. Submissions are strictly capped by your configured maximum applications per day.
